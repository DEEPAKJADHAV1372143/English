## 🔹  Context Sentences

### **Present (is / am / are)**
- *“It is a bug.”*  
- *“This is a new feature.”*  
- *“I am ready for deployment.”*  
- *“We are in the meeting.”*

### **Past (was / were)**
- *“It was a story.”*  
- *“This was a requirement.”*  
- *“I was fixing the issue.”*  
- *“They were reviewing the code.”*

### **Future (will be)**
- *“It will be a task.”*  
- *“This will be a demo.”*  
- *“I will be presenting tomorrow.”*  
- *“We will be testing next week.”*

---

## 🔹 Quick Drill
Say these aloud in sequence:
1. *“It is a bug.”*  
2. *“It was a story.”*  
3. *“It will be a task.”*

👉 This trains your tongue to switch between **present → past → future** naturally.


## 🔹 Present (have / has)
- *“I have a bug to fix.”*  
- *“We have a meeting today.”*  
- *“They have a new requirement.”*  
- *“He has a pull request.”*  
- *“She has a demo to present.”*  
- *“The project has a deadline.”*

---

## 🔹 Past (had)
- *“I had a task yesterday.”*  
- *“We had a sprint last week.”*  
- *“They had a deployment issue.”*  
- *“He had a code review.”*  
- *“She had a presentation.”*  
- *“The team had a discussion.”*

---

## 🔹 Future (will have)
- *“I will have a meeting tomorrow.”*  
- *“We will have a release next week.”*  
- *“They will have a demo soon.”*  
- *“He will have a new task.”*  
- *“She will have a training session.”*  
- *“The project will have a deadline.”*

---

## 🔹 Quick Drill
Say these aloud in sequence:
1. *“I have a bug to fix.”*  
2. *“I had a task yesterday.”*  
3. *“I will have a meeting tomorrow.”*

👉 This way you practice **have → had → will have** naturally.
